A green cube on your table

Point the phone at the table and move it slowly from side to side, about 40 cm above it. When the phone has found the table, an 8 cm green cube appears on it and stays where it is while you walk around it.

Plain or shiny wood is hard for the phone to see. Turn on a light, keep the shine off the camera, and lay a sheet of paper, a book or a cup on the table to give it something to lock onto.

Tap the screen to move the cube to where the ring is. The blue grid shows the surface the phone found; the cube sits flat on it.
