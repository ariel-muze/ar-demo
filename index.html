A green cube on any flat surface

Point the phone at a flat surface (a table, the floor, a wall, a tilted board) and move it slowly from side to side, about 40 cm away. The badge says SEARCHING while it gathers points on one surface.

When it has enough of the surface, the badge turns green, LOCKED, a yellow rectangle is drawn over the area it found, and an 8 cm green cube sits flat on the surface at the rectangle's centre. Keep sweeping and the rectangle grows over more of the surface, with the cube following its centre. Both stay put while you walk around.

Tap Find again (or the screen) to throw the lock away and search where you are aiming. Plain or shiny wood is hard to see: turn on a light and lay a sheet of paper or a book on it.
